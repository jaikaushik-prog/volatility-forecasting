Special Live Trading session on Saturday, April 28, 2012 N O T I C
E April 26, 2012 Sub: Special Live Trading session on Saturday, April 28,
2012 Trading members of the Exchange are hereby
informed that both the National Stock Exchange (NSE) & Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) have proposed to conduct
a special LIVE trading session on Saturday, April 28,
2012 from Disaster Recover site(DR) and to maintain uniformity in trading
timetable respectively. In view of the above the CSE
also proposes to enable its trading members to share trading platforms on
Saturday, April 28, 2012 of both BSE & NSE under the existing arrangement
with these Exchanges. As per the information received
from these exchanges, the session timings for the special trading on Saturday April
28, 2012 will be as follows: Session Start Time End Time Login 10:00 a.m. 11:00 a.m. Call Auction 11:00 a.m. 11:15 a.m. Continuous Trading 11:15 a.m. 12:45 p.m. Closing 12:45 p.m. 13.05 p.m. Post Closing 13.05 p.m. 13:15 p.m. Member Query and 6A / 7A entries 13:15 p.m. 13:25 p.m. The transactions done during the special trading day shall be settled under a
