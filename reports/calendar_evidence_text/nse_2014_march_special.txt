Annexure – 1 
Important Instructions regarding BCP live trading session 
 Trades done on Saturday, March 22, 2014  shall be settled on Tuesday, March 25, 2014 , as a 
separate settlement.  
 Connect2NSE facility shall be available. Extranet facility wi ll not be available from 10:30 
hrs.to 13:30 hrs. on Saturday, March 22, 2014. However, the extranet access using Internet will 
be available for the members only in download mode.  
 Co-location facility and Tick by Tick (TBT) feed shall be available. For C o-location, TBT feed 
will be available on the same Production IP's.  
 Select “BCP” radio button in TAP configuration parameters screen & save the same before 
login in BCP environment (Details as per Annexure 2). 
 For login to live environment in re-login session and from Monday March 24, 2014 onwards , 
please select the “Primary” radio button in TAP configuration parameters screen & save the 
same. (Details as per Annexure-3) 
 Please refer to the website of NSE at www.nseindia.com for any information which may be 
updated by the Exchange on the live session on March 22, 2014. 
 In case of any queries please call Toll Free no: 1800 2200 53.
